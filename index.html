How to line up two dates: 1) click the lock button so it says UNLOCKED. 2) Drag each panel until the same far away feature (bridge, pier, ridge) is in the center of both. 3) Click Pin. 4) Repeat for 2 to 4 features spread around the scene. 5) Click the lock button so it says LOCKED. Pins save for this pair of dates, and other dates line up through the pairs you have done.
