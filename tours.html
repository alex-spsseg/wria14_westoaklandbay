WRIA 14 — West Oakland Bay

360° site tours

West Oakland Bay, at the mouth of Goldsborough Creek in Shelton, is the site of a multi-phase estuary restoration led by SPSSEG to reconnect salt marsh habitat and remove historic shoreline armoring for threatened Puget Sound Chinook and steelhead, along with coho and chum. Prior phases installed engineered logjams, removed creosote pilings and bulkhead, and placed dozens of acres into permanent conservation; this final phase restores the North salt marsh lobe. This page tracks 360° site documentation from ongoing construction monitoring and drone surveys across the project area.
